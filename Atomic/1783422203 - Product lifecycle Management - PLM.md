# Product lifecycle Management - PLM

Manages the entire lifecycle of a product, from its initial concept and design to manufacturing, distribution and end-to-life disposal.

**Core functions**
- Product development
- Version control and documentation
- Compliance and quality management
- Cost Management

**Benefits**
- Enhanced collaboration: enables cross-functional teams to work together more effectively by providing a single platform for all product-related information.
- Faster Time-to-Market
- Improved Product Quality
- Cost Savings
