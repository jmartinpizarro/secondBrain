# 4PL

Unlike [[1783421538 - 3PL|3PL]] which focuses on executing logistics tasks, 4PL providers manage the entire logistics process of a company. They may act as a single point contractors for the supply chain.

**Core functions**
- Supply Chain Strategy: 4PL providers work with businesses to develop and implement supply chain and strategies that align with the company's goals and objectives.
- Coordination of Multiple 3PLs
- Technology Integration: many offer advanced technology solutions.
- End-to-End Supply Chain Management

**Benefits of 4PL**
- Holistic Supply Chain Management: companies can focused on their core business operation while ensuring that logistics are managed efficiently.
- Supply Chain Optimization: data analytics and technology for optimizing the processes
- Single Point of Contact

Examples: Accenture, DHL Supply Chain (4PL division)
