# Supplier Relationship Management - SRM

Focuses on the effective management of relationships (communications) between a company and its suppliers.

**Core functions**
- Supplier Selection and Evaluation: SRM systems help businesses evaluate and select suppliers based on various factors such as price, quality, delivery time...
- Supplier Performance Monitoring
- Contract and Procurement Management: centralize supplier contracts, purchase orders and invoices, ensuring that all procurement-related activities are tracked and compliant with company policies.
- Collaboration and Risk Management

**Benefits**
- Improved Supplier Collaboration: fosters stronger relationships between companies and their suppliers, leading to more effective communication, faster problem resolution and better alignment on shared goals.
- Cost Reduction
- Risk Mitigation
- Better decision making
