# Fundamentals of supply chain management

**End-to-end supply chain**: used for managing the flow of goods, information and finances. Six components:
1. Visibility and Transparency: ability to track and monitor all stages of the supply chain
2. Integration Across Functions: each department must collaborate and share information
3. Demand-driven Approach: chain activities are dictated by customer demand
4. Collaborative partnerships
5. Agility and flexibility
6. Sustainability and Ethics

## Outsourcing Logistics Operations

Improving efficiency and reduce cost using third party manufacturers and logistics.

- **[[1783421538 - 3PL|3PL]]** -> outsourcing specific logistics functions to an external provider. Handle physical transportation, warehousing and distribution. Not involved in the company's internal processes.
- **[[1783421542 - 4PL|4PL]]** -> manage the entire logistics process for a company. It is the single point of contact for all aspect of the chain. Operates at a more strategic level.
- **[[1783421545 - 5PL|5PL]]** -> managing e-commerce and technology driven products. Optimization is its primary goal.
