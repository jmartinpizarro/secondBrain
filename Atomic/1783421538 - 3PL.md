# 3PL

Refers to outsourcing specific logistics functions to an external provider.

**Core functions**
- Transportation
- Warehousing
- Distribution
- Freight Forwarding: involve planning and coordinating the movement of goods across international borders.
- Customs and Regulatory Compliance

**Benefits of 3PL**
- Cost Savings: business can reduce the need for in-house transportation, warehousing and logistics infrastructure.

Examples: DHL Supply Chain, XPO Logistics...
