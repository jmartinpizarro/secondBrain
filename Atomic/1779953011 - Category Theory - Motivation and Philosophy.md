# Category Theory - Motivation and Philosophy


*Almost* the most abstract part of mathematics. It is related with **[[1779953125 - Functional Programming|Functional Programming]]** and increasing the level of abstraction.

It is possible to unite almost every subject of mathematics into category theory.

Category Theory is a high-level branch of mathematics that **formalizes abstract structures and their relationships**.

Type Theory is used for solving paradoxes (e.g., You cannot create a set made of all sets).
