# 5PL

New concept in the logistics industry that focuses on managing e-commerce and technology-driven supply chains. A 5PL acts as a logistics aggregator, managing multiple 3PL and 4PL providers through digital platforms.

**Core functions**
- E-commerce logistics management
- Digital integration
- Global Supply Network Optimization
- Sustainability Initiatives

**Benefits of 5PL**
- Technology-driven solutions
- E-commerce focus
- Global Reach
- Scalability

Examples: Alibaba, Flexport, ShipBob
