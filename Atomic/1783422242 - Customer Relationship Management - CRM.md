# Customer Relationship Management - CRM

Helps businesses manage interactions with customers, clients, and sales prospects. CRM systems are designed to centralize customer data and provide businesses with a complete view of their customer relationships, from initial contact to after-sales support.

**Core functions**
- Customer data management
- Sales and opportunities tracking: CRM systems help businesses track opportunities, from lead generation to deal closure.
- Marketing automation
- Customer Support

**Benefits**
- Improved customer satisfaction
- Accurate demand forecasting
- Increased sales performance
- Better customer retention
