# Introduction to Supply Chain Management

**Supply chain**: is a series of many connected steps that a product takes from creation to when it reaches the hands of a customer.

**Supply Chain Specialist - SCS**: are responsible for coordinating and optimizing this complex network to ensure efficiency, cost-effectiveness and customer satisfaction. Their key responsibilities are:
1. **Planning and forecasting**: analyse market trends, customer demands and production capabilities. Using data, they create forecast to ensure that the right amount of product is available at the right time.
2. **Supplier Relationship Management**: negotiate contracts, manage procurement processes and ensure that suppliers deliver high-quality materials on time.
3. **Production Oversight**: once raw materials are procured, SCS oversee the manufacturing process, its scheduling aligning with respect other plans and the quality standards.
4. **Inventory and Warehousing Management**: SCSs track inventory levels, manage warehousing operations and ensure products are stored safely and efficiently.
5. **Logistics and Transportation Coordination**: SCSs optimize transportation routes, choose cost-effective shipping methods and monitor deliveries.
6. **Risk Management & Regulatory Compliance**
7. **Performance Analysis and Continuous Improvement**

**Technical Skills**:
- Data Analysis
- Supply Chain Software: enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems like Oracle or SAP.
- Project Management Tools: Microsoft Project

SCSs are vital because they ensure that:
- **Products are delivered on time**
- **Costs are controlled through efficient operations**
- **Risks are minimized through careful planning**
